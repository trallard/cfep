## Abstract

This CFEP proposes a policy for how to spend unrestricted conda-forge project funds.
It aims to provide a set of transparent principles and guidelines for decision-making
around the transparent use of administration of these funds, ensuring that they are used
in a way that benefits the conda-forge community as a whole.

## Motivation

conda-forge is a NumFOCUS (Austin, TX based non-profit 501(c)(3)) fiscally sponsored project,
as such the conda-forge project has to align with accounting and legal regulations for US-based non-profits.
Under these considerations, nonprofit donations are classified into two categories:
**unrestricted funds** which may be used for any legal purpose appropriate to the
organization and **restricted funds**, monies set aside for a particular purpose or
program (for example project-specific deliverables, educational program, etc.).

Up to now, conda-forge remains a largely volunteer-run project without significant
amounts of unrestricted funds (either from individual donations or corporate sponsorships),
thus robust policies for spending these funds have not yet been critical for the project.
However, as the conda-forge project grows there is also the potential for increased
donations and grants going directly to the project.
Therefore, it is important to establish clear, transparent, and fair principles for how
unrestricted funds can be used to support the community and its goals. Additionally,
having such principles and processes in place will make it easier to attract and manage
donations and other types of contributions moving forward.

## Scope

The following items are in scope for this CFEP:

- Principles of spending project funds (what to fund, and who to pay for this).
- Processes for decision-making around spending project funds and fund management.
- Guidelines to manage conda-forge project funds.

Out of scope items:

- Decision-making around spending project funds on a specific project or activity.
- Principles and processes for spending restricted funds, which are earmarked for
  specific purposes or projects.
  This includes most of the grant funding, which is usually earmarked for certain
  activities/deliverables and goes to an Institutional Partner rather than directly to
  the conda-forge project, and companies or institutions funding specific features.
  *For grants though, we expect the research/project leader and funded team to align their
  work with the needs of conda-forge and be receptive to feedback from conda-forge
  core team members, maintainers, and contributors.*
